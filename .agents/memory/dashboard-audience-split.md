---
name: Dashboard audience split
description: Product direction for completion-first and shooting-analysis dashboard views.
---

Keep the Dashboard organized as two swipe-only views: completion/consistency for Quick Complete players and shooting analysis for stat trackers. The top stat strip switches views; the chart area has its own section-specific carousel. Shooting has a heatmap plus one progress chart with a metric dropdown and six stat cards. The heatmap panel must scroll vertically on small screens. Training charts show shots taken by time bucket, then a visual time/workout/drill trend. Default to the saved workout logging preference. Show the Day 1 projection first in Training, using saved results or the Hub's default baseline values and visual treatment.

**Why:** The user wants Quick Complete players to focus on consistency measures without making shooting percentages the primary dashboard, while preserving detailed shooting analysis for stat trackers. They want independent swipe navigation, no duplicate metric charts, and a scrollable heatmap rather than a cropped court on mobile.

**How to apply:** Keep completion totals and the Day 1 projection distinct from shooting charts; use the saved logging preference only as the initial view, support touch plus laptop trackpad/mouse swipes without adding page-navigation buttons, and allow the heatmap panel to scroll. Match the Day 1 Hub projection's formula, colors, range bands, and axes.
