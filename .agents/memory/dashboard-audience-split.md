---
name: Dashboard audience split
description: Product direction for completion-first and shooting-analysis dashboard views.
---

Keep the Dashboard organized as two swipe-only views: completion/consistency for Quick Complete players and shooting analysis for stat trackers. The top stat strip switches views; the chart area has its own section-specific carousel. Shooting has a heatmap plus one progress chart with a metric dropdown, and retains six shooting stat cards. Avoid vertical page scrolling and extra view-navigation buttons. Default to the saved workout logging preference. Show the Day 1 projection first in Training, using saved results or the Hub's default baseline values and visual treatment.

**Why:** The user wants Quick Complete players to focus on consistency measures without making shooting percentages the primary dashboard, while preserving detailed shooting analysis for stat trackers. They also want the dashboard to fit without vertical scrolling, be navigated by independent swipes, and avoid duplicate metric charts.

**How to apply:** Keep completion totals and the Day 1 projection distinct from shooting charts; use the saved logging preference only as the initial view, and support touch plus laptop trackpad/mouse swipes without adding page-navigation buttons. Match the Day 1 Hub projection's formula, colors, range bands, and axes when presenting it on Dashboard.
