Keep workout sharing as one 9:16 image. Present the PLAYR1 mark as a square, rounded app-icon tile with a soft shadow, with player name and identity to its right. Preserve date, shot chart, and every drill. Use centered, individually outlined black recap cards with orange line icons, compact uppercase labels, and bold values; avoid orange glow. Drill rows show rounds, time, shooting, and actual non-score stats, but no score metric or overall score.

Dashboard stories may reuse the same 9:16 PLAYR1 visual direction and identity header, but their content remains period-focused summary metrics and time by category, followed by a large shooting chart at the bottom. Omit conditioning time, recent progress, and training activity.

Basketball Career, Season, and Game stories are distinct 9:16 cards. Keep the existing layout, use the app's black-and-orange style with restrained orange accents and no orange glow, and show team name rather than school name. Scale large scoring and record values to their card widths, and keep made/attempted shooting totals on one row.

**Why:** The user clarified the PLAYR1 mark should look like an app icon, then requested dashboard sharing with its own period-focused content. Later, they asked for separate Career, Season, and Game stories, then clarified that large values must fit and shooting totals must not wrap.

**How to apply:** Keep workout, dashboard, and basketball Career/Season/Game data flows separate. Dashboard stories use summary metrics, category time, and the large bottom shooting chart. Career, Season, and Game cards share the same restrained black-and-orange story styling.

For player-card sharing, use the native file share sheet when it supports both images. When it does not, show direct, user-clicked download links for the front and back instead of relying on scripted multi-file downloads or claiming they succeeded.

**Why:** Embedded previews and browsers may block background downloads or native file sharing, leaving only a brief loading state and no usable images.

**How to apply:** Keep a visible fallback available for unsupported or rejected native sharing; cancellation stays distinct and must not be treated as a completed share.
