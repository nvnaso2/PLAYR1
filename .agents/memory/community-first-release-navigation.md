---
name: Community first-release navigation
description: First-release navigation scope for the Community feature.
---

Keep the Community pages and behavior intact for future reintroduction. For the first version, omit Community from player bottom navigation and expose its sidebar entry only to admins.

**Why:** The user wants the first version focused and centralized while preserving the Community experience for later restoration.

**How to apply:** Hide or relocate Community navigation without deleting or rewriting its routes and page code. Keep the admin-only sidebar entry until the user changes this product decision.
