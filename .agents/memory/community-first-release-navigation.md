---
name: Community first-release navigation
description: First-release navigation scope for the Community feature.
---

Keep the Community pages and behavior intact for future reintroduction. For the first version, omit Community from player bottom navigation and expose its main sidebar entry only to admins. Give Community 3 Pointers submissions their own separate admin menu entry, outside Player Inbox.

**Why:** The user wants the first version focused and centralized while preserving the Community experience for later restoration, and asked for Community 3 Pointers to be separate from player messaging.

**How to apply:** Hide or relocate Community navigation without deleting or rewriting its routes and page code. Keep the admin-only Community and Community 3 Pointers destinations until the user changes this product decision.
