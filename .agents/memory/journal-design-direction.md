---
name: Journal design direction
description: The user's preferred structure and visual direction for the PLAYR1 Journal.
---

Treat Journal as an electronic notebook. The default view has a starter prompt printed on the page, with Free write as a fourth choice alongside Practice, Game, and Open. Keep Analyze and Complete compact at the top, use the Coach P symbol for analysis, omit extra header/intro/privacy copy and Add details, use a black outer background, and present Past Entries as a clear archive action. Use the existing prompt library, respect Coach P's Pro boundary, and prefill type and source when arriving from a workout or game.

**Why:** The user wants Journal to feel like a real but electronic notebook: simple, concise, clean, and functional, with old entries separated from the writing page.

**How to apply:** Keep the prompted notebook page as the landing view, place the prompt on the paper itself, keep the four writing modes together, put compact Analyze and Complete actions at the top, put saved-entry search and filters behind Past Entries, preserve legacy responses, and keep AI analysis and conversational Coach P within the existing Pro boundary.
