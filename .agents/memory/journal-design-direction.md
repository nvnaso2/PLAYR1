---
name: Journal design direction
description: The user's preferred structure and visual direction for the PLAYR1 Journal.
---

Treat Journal as a private electronic notebook. The default view is a blank writing page with a starter prompt printed on the notebook; offer Free write as a fourth choice alongside Practice, Game, and Open. Keep Analyze and Save compact at the top, omit the extra page header, intro copy, and Add details control, use a black outer background, and keep saved entries behind one Past Entries button. Use the existing prompt library, respect Coach P's Pro boundary, and prefill type and source when arriving from a workout or game.

**Why:** The user wants Journal to feel like a real but electronic notebook: simple, concise, clean, and functional, with old entries separated from the writing page.

**How to apply:** Keep the prompted notebook page as the landing view, place the prompt on the paper itself, keep the four writing modes together, put compact Analyze and Save actions at the top, put saved-entry search and filters behind Past Entries, preserve legacy responses, and keep AI analysis and conversational Coach P within the existing Pro boundary.
