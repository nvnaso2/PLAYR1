name: Journal design direction
description: The user's preferred structure and visual direction for the PLAYR1 Journal.
---

For Journal, keep the main page concise around three jobs: start a new reflection, find past reflections, and open a saved reflection for Coach P review. Use PLAYR1's charcoal/zinc surfaces and orange accents; avoid long hero or explanation blocks and mismatched pale-green/beige styling.

**Why:** The user said the previous redesign felt too long, cluttered, and disconnected from the app's colors, and named those three jobs as the core of the page.

**How to apply:** Keep saved reflections on the main page, make starting a reflection a clear action, preserve Coach P on each reflection within the existing Pro boundary, and collapse secondary filters or controls.
