---
name: Journal design direction
description: The user's preferred structure and visual direction for the PLAYR1 Journal.
---

Treat Journal as a private electronic notebook: make writing the primary action, with concise saved pages that are easy to find and reopen. Keep free writing first and mood, source links, and prompts optional. Use PLAYR1 charcoal/zinc surfaces and orange accents; avoid clutter and long promotional copy.

**Why:** The user wants Journal to feel like a real but electronic notebook: simple, concise, clean, and functional.

**How to apply:** Keep saved entries on the main page, make starting a page a clear action, preserve legacy responses and Coach P within the existing Pro boundary, and collapse secondary filters or controls.
