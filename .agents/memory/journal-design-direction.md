---
name: Journal design direction
description: The user's preferred structure and visual direction for the PLAYR1 Journal.
---

Treat Journal as an electronic notebook. The default view has a starter prompt printed on the page, with Practice, Game, Open, and Free write in an icon-labeled dropdown. Put Past Entries at the right, keep Complete inside the paper, and make Coach P a symbol-only button that opens a short explanation before analysis. Do not add an extra bubble around the Coach P symbol; use the Coach P mark rather than sparkles for Coach P actions. Style Past Entries with the same cream notebook-paper aesthetic, and limit its filters to date and type. Omit extra header/intro/privacy copy and Add details; use a black outer background. Use the existing prompt library, respect Coach P's Pro boundary, and prefill type and source when arriving from a workout or game.

**Why:** The user wants Journal to feel like a real but electronic notebook: simple, concise, clean, and functional, with old entries separated from the writing page and archive filters kept simple.

**How to apply:** Keep the prompted notebook page as the landing view, place the prompt on the paper itself, use the icon-labeled selector for writing modes, align the archive action to the right, place Complete in the page footer, style the archive surface like the notebook paper, limit archive filters to date/type, preserve legacy responses, and keep AI analysis and conversational Coach P within the existing Pro boundary.
