---
name: Auth and launch visual direction
description: User-directed visual constraints for PLAYR1 sign-in and the startup splash.
---

For PLAYR1, sign-in should remain intentionally minimal: a centered logo and email/password fields, warm orange glowing pill fields, an orange Pro-colored Sign In action, and inline Forgot Password and New Profile links. Avoid marketing copy and hero imagery on the sign-in view. Keep the startup splash as the initial preview screen with a black background, logo, glowing animated progress, and a Start Your PLAYR1 Journey action.

**Why:** the user asked for a simpler, centered sign-in and a more polished black launch screen to test.

**How to apply:** When revising these surfaces, preserve the sparse composition and splash-first entry; do not add sign-in content or bypass the startup flow unless the user asks.
