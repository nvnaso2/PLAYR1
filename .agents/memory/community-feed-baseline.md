name: Community feed baseline
description: The current Community activity feed treatment and rolling time window.
---

The approved baseline is a compact, straight newest-first activity list with consistent thin horizontal cards, a restrained event-color rail, and fully visible stats aligned on the right. Show 10 items initially and reveal 10 more with a bottom button.

**Why:** The user rejected grouped day toggles and prefers one fast-scanning chronological list with controlled length.

**How to apply:** Keep newest-first ordering and the rolling seven-day window. Never truncate activity stats or let them overflow. Keep stat badges on the right and paginate visually in batches of 10 with “See more.”
