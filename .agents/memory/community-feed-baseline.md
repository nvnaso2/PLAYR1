---
name: Community feed baseline
description: The current Community activity feed treatment and rolling time window.
---

The approved baseline is a compact multi-item activity feed with consistent card treatment, a restrained event-color rail, and fully visible stats that wrap inside each card. Activity from the latest 72 hours stays expanded; older activity is grouped by calendar day and collapsed by default.

**Why:** The user prefers quick scanning without clipped details and assumes updates older than 72 hours have usually already been seen.

**How to apply:** Keep newest-first ordering and the rolling seven-day window. Never truncate activity stats or let them overflow. Group only items older than 72 hours into individually expandable day sections.
