Journal reflections remain available to every player, but any Journal Coach P feedback or in-entry conversation must be rejected server-side for non-Pro users and must not increment or refund the standalone Coach P allowance.

**Why:** The Journal is a retention and development workflow, so removing ordinary reflection for free players is too restrictive; mixing its AI usage into the limited standalone allowance also makes entitlement behavior confusing.

**How to apply:** Keep the entitlement check before any provider call, return a stable upgrade response, and route Journal context through the shared Coach P assembly without charging the non-Pro counter.
