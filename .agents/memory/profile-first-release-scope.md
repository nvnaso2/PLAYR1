For the first Profile version, hide My Season, My PLAYR1 Project, and Game Day from player-facing entry points, but preserve their pages and routes for later. Keep My Profile and My Career separate until the user decides what information should be combined.

The Profile page is the primary PLAYR1 Pro value and upgrade surface: keep its Free-vs-Pro summary, value comparison, and current-plan status inline rather than sending players to a separate marketing page. Keep the three Profile shortcuts compact and icon-led, with the 3 Point Challenge orange/play-inspired, Journal notebook-like and non-purple, and Talk Through Your Game as an orange glowing chat icon.

**Why:** The user wants the Profile/Career decision deferred and specified that Pro details and the three shortcuts should be presented directly on Profile.

**How to apply:** When revisiting Profile, preserve the inline upgrade surface and shortcut treatment. Do not merge or remove Career details based on assumptions; ask for the intended summary and preserve the current structure until then.
