---
name: My Season eligibility
description: Product rule for deciding who needs season and Game Day context
---

My Season is an optional competitive-basketball layer. Its first question asks whether the player participates in or is preparing for competitive basketball, including intramural, school, travel/club, recreational teams, or tryout preparation. Players who play only for fun should not be pushed through team, season, or event setup.

**Why:** Season management adds unnecessary friction for casual players, while competitive players need calendar-aware In Season, Off Season, and Game Day context.

**How to apply:** Persist the answer. A true answer starts the guided team setup; a false answer leaves the player in the regular Train experience and offers a way to change the answer later.
