---
name: Workout logging preference
description: The account-level default between Quick Complete and Track Stats.
---

After completing the Day 3 onboarding workout, ask the player whether they prefer Quick Complete or Track Stats. Save the choice as the player's account default and include current preference totals in admin training analytics.

**Why:** Players may want to track training consistency and volume without entering shooting percentages; preference should guide the default mode rather than remove access to either mode.

**How to apply:** Use the saved choice when starting later workouts unless the player explicitly selects a mode for that session. Keep both modes available.
