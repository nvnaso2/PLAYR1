---
name: Settings family controls
description: Scope decision for family and parent-invitation controls in PLAYR1 Settings.
---

The Settings page should not show Family & Privacy or parent-invitation controls. Remove Settings-only entry points and UI state; do not delete parental routes, APIs, or stored data without a separate request.

**Why:** The user asked to remove the Family & Privacy area from Settings, not to retire parental features or erase related data.

**How to apply:** Keep the Settings page focused on notifications and account actions while preserving unrelated parent and family workflows. Reintroduce these controls in Settings only if the user explicitly asks.
