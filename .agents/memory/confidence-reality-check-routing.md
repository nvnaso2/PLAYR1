---
name: Confidence quiz routing
description: Product decision for how the Confidence Reality Check handles its opening yes/no answer.
---

In the Confidence Reality Check, Question 1 is self-reflection. Both “Yes” and “No” continue to Question 2; neither answer routes into a different diagnostic.

**Why:** The product owner clarified that the first answer is for players to reflect on their confidence, not a branching rule. This supersedes the source copy's Question 1 “No” route to Advanced Pressure & Game Conversion.

**How to apply:** Keep Question 1 answer tracking for trend reporting, but do not let it change quiz navigation. The reps answer in Question 2 determines whether the quiz returns the volume-work result or continues to the pressure-specific question.
