---
name: Shooting Cohort
description: Product rules for Shooting Cohort intake, workouts, messaging, and admin management
---

Shooting Cohort is the Hub entry to a free, ongoing founder-led shooting cohort. Any player may apply regardless of age. Admit applicants in application order until the admin-set capacity fills; after that, the admin manually invites players from the waitlist.

Accepted members get a dedicated portal and individually assigned workouts. The application asks about practice days and hoop access, session length, weekly shot volume, one or two focus areas, confidence, a goal, and optional routine notes. The baseline is 100 threes (20 from each of five spots), 40 mid-range pull-ups (8 from each spot), and 50 free throws. Show dated progress for those three categories in Baseline Assessment; cohort admins can set a goal percentage for each. Suggest retests every four weeks.

Manage roster, capacity, waitlist, and results in its own Shooting Cohort admin destination, not inside Player Inbox. Admin views are Cohort, Applications, Film Review, and Custom Requests. Show outstanding counts for applications needing review or waitlisted, active-member film submissions without feedback, and submitted/in-progress custom requests; show their sum beside Shooting Cohort in the admin menu. Film Review lists active members' private submissions and lets the Founder leave feedback visible to the submitting player. There is no shared cohort chat; Messaging is only the private player-Founder conversation. Preserve historical group-message records, but do not expose them in the cohort UI or API. Needs Review is assigned manually; Accepted and Invited reserve seats; preserve oldest-first automatic admission. Free cohort membership unlocks cohort-related Custom Requests even though the general Custom Requests feature remains Pro-gated. Keep application answers admin-only; players see their shooting results and dated progress in Baseline Assessment. Match cohort administration to the dashboard's dark, neutral, orange-accented UI while preserving counts, controls, and oldest-first application order.

No guardian consent rule is required for under-18 membership or messaging.

The Hub tool is named “Shooting Cohort.” The player-facing title is “Build an Effortless Shot” in white. Show the number of active cohort members, not remaining capacity, and keep Founder-led / Measurable Results highlights. Do not add a separate Message Founder button. The active player tabs are Workouts, Film Room, Messaging, and Baseline Assessment; omit a general Analytics tab. Workouts shows assigned workouts and requested workouts, never assigned drills; once a request is assigned, remove it from the request list and show its workout only once among assignments. Assigned workouts must open the workout player. The request action goes directly to the form. The request form multi-selects skills, captures ideal duration and specific improvement details, shows the saved hoop-access answer as equipment context, and optionally attaches a private video.

Keep the “Request a workout” action visible and enabled across all member tabs, including while another request is active. Opening it should switch to Workouts and show the cohort request form.

Active applicants and members may edit their interest-survey answers without changing their application status. Keep cohort video submissions separate from the general Film Room; playback is limited to the active member and authorized admin reviewer through short-lived URLs. Founder feedback must only be visible to the submitting player.

Remove the active-status “You’re In” card and place “Edit Interest Survey” in the header action row. Keep member tools behind the active-membership check.

**Why:** The user specified these cohort access, navigation, messaging, privacy, video-review, survey-editing, and visual rules.

**How to apply:** Preserve these choices when designing cohort intake, tabs, entitlements, assignments, messaging, video uploads, request access, and admin management. Do not change the general Pro boundary for players outside the cohort.
