---
name: Shooting Cohort
description: Product rules for Shooting Cohort intake, workouts, messaging, and admin management
---

Shooting Cohort is the Hub entry to a free, ongoing founder-led shooting cohort. Any player may apply regardless of age. Admit applicants in application order until the admin-set capacity fills; after that, the admin manually invites players from the waitlist.

Accepted members get a dedicated portal and individually assigned workouts. The user approved the first survey: practice days and access, session length, weekly shot volume, one or two focus areas, confidence, a goal, and optional routine notes. The approved baseline is 20 free throws, 25 spot-up attempts across five locations, and 15 off-dribble attempts across three locations; record distance and suggest retests every four weeks.

Manage roster, capacity, waitlist, and results in its own Shooting Cohort admin destination, not inside Player Inbox. Player Inbox is only for private player conversations. Give Community 3 Pointers a separate admin destination outside Player Inbox, and keep Custom Requests as its own view inside Shooting Cohort. Keep private player-Founder threads and one shared chat for all cohort members. Split cohort administration into Cohort (program controls and stats), Applications (Accepted, Invited, Waitlisted, Needs Review), Shared Conversation, and Custom Requests views. Needs Review is assigned manually; Accepted and Invited reserve seats; preserve oldest-first automatic admission. Free cohort membership unlocks cohort-related Custom Requests even though the general Custom Requests feature remains Pro-gated. Keep application answers and shooting results admin-only. Match cohort administration to the dashboard's dark, neutral, orange-accented UI while preserving counts, controls, and oldest-first application order.

No guardian consent rule is required for under-18 membership or messaging.

The Hub tool is named “Shooting Cohort.” The player-facing page keeps “Build an Effortless Shot,” Remaining Spots, Interest Survey and Founder-message actions, and Founder-led / Measurable Results highlights, but has no P1/PLAYR1 brand stack, duplicate cohort eyebrow, or supporting coaching tagline at the top. The Founder DM should show the Founder’s name with a small message icon, retain “Direct Message to the Founder” and “Talk Through Your Game,” omit the long intro, and provide a back link to the cohort.

**Why:** The user specified these cohort, access, messaging, privacy, navigation, and visual rules.

**How to apply:** Preserve these choices when designing cohort intake, entitlements, assignments, messaging, and admin management. Do not change the general Pro boundary for players outside the cohort.
