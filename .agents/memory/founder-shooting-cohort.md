---
name: Founder-led shooting cohort
description: Product rules for the Talk Through Your Game shooting cohort, intake, workouts, messaging, and admin management
---

Talk Through Your Game is the entry to a free, ongoing founder-led shooting cohort. Any player may apply regardless of age. Admit applicants in application order until the admin-set capacity fills; after that, the admin manually invites players from the waitlist.

Accepted members get a dedicated portal and individually assigned workouts. The user approved the first survey: practice days and access, session length, weekly shot volume, one or two focus areas, confidence, a goal, and optional routine notes. The approved baseline is 20 free throws, 25 spot-up attempts across five locations, and 15 off-dribble attempts across three locations; record distance and suggest retests every four weeks.

Manage roster, capacity, waitlist, and results in a Shooting Cohort tab within Player Inbox. Keep private player-Founder threads and one shared chat for all cohort members. Free cohort membership unlocks cohort-related Custom Requests even though the general Custom Requests feature remains Pro-gated. Keep shooting results private to each player and admins.

No guardian consent rule is required for under-18 membership or messaging.

**Why:** The user specified these cohort, access, messaging, and privacy rules.

**How to apply:** Preserve these choices when designing cohort intake, entitlements, assignments, messaging, and admin management. Do not change the general Pro boundary for players outside the cohort.
