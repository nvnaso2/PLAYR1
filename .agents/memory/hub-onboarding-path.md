The normal production Hub remains automatic and profile-completion-based. Onboarding contains the intro trailer, a title-only Complete Your Profile action linking to Edit Profile, and the shared training-start choices. Everyday contains the training snapshot, exactly three catalog-based Custom Drills suggestions selected from profile focus, experience, and position, plus PLAYR1 Tools. Replace the Custom Workout choice with one setup-informed Coach P question in the shared training grid in both states; do not also show a standalone Coach P card. Drill suggestions are deterministic, do not call AI, and rotate by local calendar day. The welcome-workout chat may offer a temporary onboarding Hub preview; it must not change the player's saved profile or normal Hub state, and the preview needs a clear return to the normal Hub.

Development may expose only Onboarding and Everyday as QA state choices; the initial state remains profile-derived. The post-setup welcome action opens a Coach P conversation with a personalized snapshot from completed setup answers and relevant saved lead-magnet answers, followed by an immediate catalog-backed starter workout. Players can continue chatting, explicitly save and start the workout, or open the temporary onboarding Hub preview. Keep the old welcome-workout route as a redirect, not a separate recap or auto-save path.

**Why:** The user wants profile-completion-based Hub states to remain automatic, with one Coach P question in the shared training grid and daily drill suggestions chosen without AI. The welcome-workout preview must remain temporary and separate from saved profile state.

**How to apply:** Keep the default Hub profile-derived and QA controls gated to development builds with only Onboarding and Everyday choices. Keep the Coach P tile in the Custom Workout position in the shared training list, display its prompt in a compact chat-style bubble, and remove any duplicate Coach P section. Keep exactly three available public catalog drills in the daily suggestions when the library has at least three eligible entries; rotate deterministically using profile context and local date. Allow the onboarding preview only through its explicit welcome-chat action, and keep the real profile state unchanged.
