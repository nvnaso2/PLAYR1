14-Day Program Structure
Phase 1: The Mechanical Foundation (Days 1–4)
Day 1: Ground Force & Balance

Video: Using the balls of your feet as a springboard; maintaining vertical axis balance under fatigue.

Drill Assignment: Base Balance Warm-up & 100-Make Springboard Progression.

Day 2: Explosive Sequencing & The Dip

Video: Hips dropping, ball moving first, loading the elbow/hand under the ball at lightning speed.

Drill Assignment: Energy Transfer Reps (Low-Hips to Fast-Pocket Lift).

Day 3: The Frictionless Follow-Through

Video: Guide-hand release, wrist snap, clean backspin, and holding your finish.

Drill Assignment: Straight-Line Spin Test & 50 Swishes Only.

Day 4: Mechanics Lock-In & Testing

Video: Putting Balance + Sequencing + Follow Through into a single continuous motion.

Drill Assignment: The Mechanical Benchmark Test (Logging initial baseline percentages).

Phase 2: Dynamic Movement & Space Creation (Days 5–8)
Day 5: The 5 Essential Shot-Creation Moves

Video: Deep dive into your 5 core moves off the bounce, maintaining base balance on every pickup.

Drill Assignment: The 5-Move Matrix (10 Makes per move from 3 spots).

Day 6: Off-Ball Catch & Footwork Mechanics

Video: 1-2 steps, hop pickups, and aligning your body to the rim out of full-sprint movement.

Drill Assignment: Off-Ball Relocation Shooting & Sweep-and-Sway Reps.

Day 7: Dynamic Pickups & Ball Handling

Video: Clean pocket gathers, high-to-low transitions, and eliminating wasted motion off the dribble.

Drill Assignment: Heavy-Ball / High-Speed Gather Series.

Day 8: Space Creation Combine

Video: Combining off-ball catches and on-ball creation in a continuous circuit.

Drill Assignment: 150-Make Dynamic Movement Circuit.

Phase 3: High-Volume Tracking & Mindset (Days 9–11)
Day 9: The Mindset of the Streak

Video: Psychological framing of making shots in a row; overcoming the fear of missing after 3 consecutive makes.

Drill Assignment: "Streak 5" Challenge (Must hit 5 in a row from 5 spots to advance).

Day 10: The Warm-up Ritual & Flow State

Video: The exact mental and physical routine to unlock confidence before taking a single game-speed jump shot.

Drill Assignment: The Pre-Workout Activation Protocol.

Day 11: Data & Volume Tracking

Video: How to track makes vs. misses systematically to build undeniable self-trust.

Drill Assignment: The 200-Make Metric Log (In-app tracking input).

Phase 4: Game-Speed Conversion & Mastery (Days 12–14)
Day 12: High-Fatigue Conversion

Video: Keeping your balance and sequencing intact when your legs are burning.

Drill Assignment: Sprint-to-Shot Burnout Drills.

Day 13: Game-Simulation Testing

Video: Simulating real defensive pressure, contested vision, and quick-trigger releases.

Drill Assignment: The Game-Speed Benchmark Test.

Day 14: The Final 100 & Graduation

Video: How to maintain this standard for 200+ days; integrating the system into your off-season.

Drill Assignment: The Adaptive Shot Final Challenge.
