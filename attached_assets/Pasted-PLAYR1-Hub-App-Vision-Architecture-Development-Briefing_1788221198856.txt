PLAYR1 Hub — App Vision, Architecture & Development Briefing
1. Product Overview & Vision
PLAYR1 Hub is the central digital ecosystem for modern basketball players seeking high-level player development, structured training, and career tracking.

The app operates on a three-pillar user journey:

Diagnose & Improve (Free): Diagnostic quizzes identify specific mechanical or tactical flaws, pairing users with targeted single drills to fix their game. Serves as the primary top-of-funnel lead magnet.

Learn & Coach (Paid): Full masterclass-style courses, structured multi-week programs, and an AI basketball coach (Coach P) for personalized guidance.

Track & Share (Free / Growth): Dynamic workout and game stat tracking, shooting heatmaps, player profiles, and social features to drive community retention and viral sharing.

2. Core Tab Architecture & Navigation
The app is structured around a 5-tab bottom navigation bar:

┌───────────┬───────────┬───────────┬───────────┬───────────┐
│   Train   │  Compete  │    Hub    │ Community │  Profile  │
└───────────┴───────────┴───────────┴───────────┴───────────┘
1. Train Tab
Primary Actions: Start Workout, Create Drill, Create Workout, Create Challenge, Free Shoot mode.

Sections:

Active Challenges: User's active streak/training challenges.

Daily Free Drills: Rotating single drills for non-paying users.

Drill Library: Searchable/filterable database of individual drills.

Programs & Courses (Gated - Paid Tier): Multi-week video/text courses (e.g., Jump Shot Mastery, Vertical Boost).

2. Compete Tab
Primary Focus: Live 3-Point Challenge matching creator video challenges.

Features:

Live streak tracking (recording 3-point makes in a row).

Drill and challenge leaderboards.

3. Hub Tab (Main Dashboard)
Primary Focus: The central command center customized to the player's diagnostic data.

Features:

Summary analytics & streak status.

Suggested daily drills/programs based on onboarding quiz and profile results.

Quick-launch buttons for logging workouts and accessing the Drill Library.

Coach P AI Entry Point: Direct entry to chat with the AI basketball coach for custom workout generation and Q&A.

Creator Feed / Direct Announcement System.

4. Community Tab
Features:

Friends list and activity feed.

Group Challenges.

Find Players (local pickup/training partner matching).

Note: Remove legacy sections ("My Trainer", "Find Teams & Leagues") to streamline the UI.

5. Profile Tab ("My Career")
Features:

Player Profile card (Archetype, Position, Baseline Percentile Score).

Saved Diagnostic Quiz Results.

Detailed Game & Workout Analytics (including Dynamic Shooting Heatmaps).

Goal Setting & Tracking.

Game Day UI Mode: Pre-game routine builder and scheduling assistant.

3. Monetization & Funnel Model
Free Tier: Access to Diagnostic Quizzes, Daily Free Drills, Workout/Game Stat Tracking, Shooting Heatmaps, Profile Creation, and Community features.

Paid Tier (Monthly/Annual Subscription): Unlocks full access to all Programs & Courses, Coach P AI (chat + custom workout generator), advanced analytics, and exclusive challenges.

4. Technical Feature Backlog & Release Roadmap
Phase 1: Funnel & Core Infrastructure (Immediate Priority)
Onboarding & Diagnostic Engine: Build dynamic quiz UI on first launch; store diagnostic output directly to user profile.

Subscription & Payment Gate: Integrate RevenueCat/Stripe/In-App Purchases to lock Programs/Courses in the Train tab and Coach P AI in the Hub behind a subscription wall.

Navigation Cleanup: Remove outdated UI elements from the Community tab (My Trainer, Teams & Leagues).

Phase 2: Engagement & Value Add (v1.1 - v1.2)
Coach P AI Integration: Conversational AI trained on basketball coaching methodology with custom workout generation capabilities.

Dynamic Shooting Heatmap: Visual Court Map rendering make/miss percentages by court zones.

Game Day UI Mode: Dedicated low-friction interface for game days featuring pre-game routines, mental prep checklists, and stat logging.

Goal Setting & Journaling: Interactive goal-tracking engine with post-workout reflection prompts.

Push Notifications: Automated daily workout and streak maintenance reminders.

Phase 3: Virality & Optimization (v1.3)
Shareable Progress Graphics: Auto-generate branded, aesthetic summary graphics ("Post to Story" format) for Instagram/TikTok.

In-Season vs. Offseason Modes: Dynamic UI switch adjusting training volume and drill recommendations based on season timing.

5. Immediate Development Sprint Tasks
Database & Auth Setup: Ensure user profiles persist diagnostic quiz responses and user state.

Subscription Infrastructure: Set up paywall screens and logic for gating course content.

Hub UI Polish: Wire the main dashboard to dynamically display diagnostic-driven drill recommendations.

Course Viewer UI: Build a video-player container and module breakdown UI for structured training programs inside the Train tab.
