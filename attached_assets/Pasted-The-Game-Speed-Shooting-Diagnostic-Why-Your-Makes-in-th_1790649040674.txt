The Game-Speed Shooting Diagnostic
Why Your Makes in the Empty Gym Aren’t Translating to 5v5
Most players don't have a shooting problem—they have a conversion problem. When your mechanics are pure in open warmups but collapse in real runs, the breakdown always stems from one of three friction points: Fatigue, Simulation, or Pressure.
Take this 30-second assessment to find your primary conversion leak and unlock the exact drill-and-cue protocol to bridge the gap.

Question 1: Which condition makes it the hardest to perform in the 4th Quarter of a game?
A) My legs are heavy at the end of the game and struggle to have the necessary conditioning and energy to perform
B) The defense gets intense causing me to rush and miss difficult shots I haven't practiced enough
C) The pressure of the game increases causing me to stiffen up or other think the situation and shots I am taking
Question 2: In what environment do you perform at your worst when shooting?
A) When you're tired or fatigued
B) Taking game like reps while moving rather than simple stationary reps
C) Under high pressures or pressure shots

Question 3: What is the biggest gap between your training sessions and real 5v5 games?

A) Intensity: My workouts involve too much standing around and resting between makes compared to a real games pace
B) Footwork & Drill Diversity: In my workouts, I shoot simple and basic shots. In games I have to shoot while dribbling downhill and or coming off screens
C) Accountability: In workouts, missing has zero consequences. In the games every miss feels costly and holds a lot of weight.

Majority A's: You are a Fatigue-Limited Shooter.
Majority B's: You are a Simulation-Limited Shooter.
Majority C's: You are a Pressure-Limited Shooter.
